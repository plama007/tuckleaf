Privacy policy

Tuckleaf collects no data about you. Last updated 18 October 2026.

Tuckleaf is a notebook for words you meet while reading. It has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it makes no network connections of its own.

Data not collected. Tuckleaf collects no data about you. We have no servers and receive nothing from the app. There is nothing to request, correct or delete beyond what you can delete yourself: removing the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone, except the small count of free words used (see Purchases).

What stays on your iPhone. The words you save, the lines and books you attach, your notes, your practice history and your preferences are stored on your device in Tuckleaf's own database. Nothing is sent to us.

iCloud sync (optional). If your version of Tuckleaf has iCloud sync and you are signed in to iCloud, your words are stored in your own private iCloud database, which only your devices and Apple's iCloud service can read. We cannot see it. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for Tuckleaf in the iPhone's Settings app.

Purchases. Tuckleaf Lifetime, the one-time unlock, is bought through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple handles the payment; we receive no payment details, name or email. The app keeps only a note on your device that the unlock is owned, and a count of the free words used so far (also kept in your iPhone's Keychain so it survives reinstalling the app; it never leaves the device).

Microphone. "Say it" on a practice card or a word's page records a few seconds of your voice so you can compare it with the spoken word. For a saved word, your latest recording is kept on this iPhone so you can hear it again; the next one replaces it, and it is deleted with the word. It is not synced or included in iCloud backups, and it never leaves your iPhone. A recording of a word you have not saved is deleted when you leave it.

Camera. Scanning a line from a printed page uses the camera to recognise text on the device. No photo or video is saved.

Kindle import. A Kindle file you choose is read on the device to find words and sentences; a temporary copy is deleted when the import finishes.

Spotlight. Saved words are indexed on your device so iPhone search can find them. The index stays on the device and is removed with the app.

Apple Intelligence. On iPhones that have it, a few optional extras ("In plain words", translation) run on the device through Apple's frameworks. Nothing is sent to us.

Dictionary. The dictionary is built into the app from Wiktionary (CC BY-SA 4.0); looking words up needs no connection.

Children. Tuckleaf collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address with a new date.
