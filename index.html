Questions

What is free, and what does Tuckleaf Lifetime open?

Free, for as long as you like: looking up any word, saving your first 25 words (and viewing, practising and exporting every word you save), the 16 grammar steps, the verb tables, the Vocabulary list, a sample of every library and each day's idiom, word pair, grammar topic and everyday phrase. Tuckleaf Lifetime is one purchase that opens everything else: unlimited saved words, every idiom, every word pair, every grammar topic and every everyday situation. Pay once. No subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Tuckleaf, then tap Restore purchase on the unlock page or in Settings. Your purchase comes back at no charge.

Can my family use my purchase?

Yes. Tuckleaf Lifetime supports Family Sharing: up to five family members in your Apple family can use it on their own iPhones. Turn on Purchase Sharing in the iPhone's Settings › your name › Family Sharing.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a refund is granted, Lifetime closes again, but your words and everything you saved stay open.

Where are my words kept?

On your iPhone. Tuckleaf has no account and no servers, and it sends nothing to us. If your version has iCloud sync and you are signed in to iCloud, your words also go to your own private iCloud, which we cannot see.

Can I take my words out?

Yes, always, free or not. In Settings, choose Export as CSV (for a spreadsheet) or Export as JSON. Your words, meanings, lines and books go with them.

Does it need the internet?

No. The full dictionary is on your iPhone. Only buying or restoring Tuckleaf Lifetime talks to the App Store.

Where do the definitions come from?

From Wiktionary, under CC BY-SA 4.0. The full attribution is in Settings › Attributions.
